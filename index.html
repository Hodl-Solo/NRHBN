Why a network?

Local first.
Connected by Bitcoin.

The Northern Rivers already has several independent Bitcoin communities. The Honey Badger Network gives them a shared front door without turning them into one central organisation.

Meetups stay independent. The network simply makes it easier to discover what is happening nearby, share events and help new groups get started.
